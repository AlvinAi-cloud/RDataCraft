# RDataCraft Code of Conduct

RDataCraft welcomes contributors and users of different backgrounds, identities,
abilities and levels of experience. This code applies to project issues, pull
requests, reviews and other project-related interactions.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and patience. Give newcomers room to learn.
- Discuss evidence, designs and code; make criticism specific and constructive.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution and intellectual property.
- Describe scientific claims honestly, including uncertainty and limitations.
- Accept corrections and help resolve disagreements without personal attacks.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing and repeated disruptive or abusive behavior are not
acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information or study data
without their permission. Good-faith disagreement about methods or implementation
is welcome when expressed respectfully.

## Reporting

Contact [Huiwang Ai / AlvinAi-cloud](https://github.com/AlvinAi-cloud), the project
maintainer, through an available private contact channel. If none is available,
open an [issue](https://github.com/AlvinAi-cloud/RDataCraft/issues) titled
**Code of Conduct: private contact requested**, with no incident details or
personal information. The maintainer can then arrange a private reporting
channel. Do not post sensitive allegations publicly to request help.

Provide the relevant context and evidence privately. Reports will be handled
with discretion; information should be shared only as needed to investigate and
respond. Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may clarify expectations, request a correction, remove inappropriate
content, temporarily restrict participation or block participation, depending on
severity and repetition. They should explain the reason where practical and
apply these expectations consistently. A participant may request reconsideration
through the same reporting channel. Maintainers are subject to this code too.
